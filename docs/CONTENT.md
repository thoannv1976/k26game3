not an official VSTEP exam; `conversion` is empty until the school approves a table.

`packages/game-engine/src/check.ts`). Names and organisations are fictional. Listening voices: Google Neural2 en-GB/en-US/en-AU.
New items: Admin → Content → AI generator (always a draft) or JSON editor; must pass the checks before approval.
